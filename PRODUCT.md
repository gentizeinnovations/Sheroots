# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Women in and around Tamil Nadu (mixed needs: everyday pain and injury, pregnancy, postnatal recovery, pelvic health), usually searching on a phone and reading in English and Tamil. They are ready to call or book once they trust the clinic.

## Product Purpose
Website for SHEroots Physiotherapy Clinic. It explains treatments and moves a visitor to call or book a session. Success is a phone call or booking.

## Positioning
Women-first, root-cause care: a calm, private space where patients can speak freely, with women's health specialisms (pelvic floor, pregnancy, postnatal, PCOS/PCOD, diastasis recti, incontinence) alongside general physiotherapy. Treats why it hurts, not only the symptom.

## Operating Context
Clinic open 9 AM to 1 PM and 5 PM to 9 PM. 19 treatment pages (12 core, 7 women's health) in `treatments/`. Pages are generated by a Python build (`_build/build.py`, `_build/content.py`) into static HTML; edit the build sources, not only the output. Includes a chat widget (`chat.js`) and daily reports in `reports/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Bilingual content (English with Tamil headings and hours).
- Static HTML/CSS/JS site; no framework.
- Stack and visual system already exist (incumbent implementation).

## Brand Commitments
Name styled "SHEroots" (the "SHE" emphasised). Tagline "Strong roots. Stronger you." Strapline "Move well. Live strong. Rooted in you." Logo assets in `images/` (logo-figure, logo-left, logo-right).

## Evidence on Hand
No real proof available yet. The "Trusted & recognised" strip and Google review blocks currently show sample entries, labelled as previews (real ones are set at the top of `script.js`). Future work must not fabricate testimonials, credentials, counts or awards.

## Product Principles
- Make calling or booking the easiest action on every page, especially on mobile.
- Comfort and privacy first: tone is calm and respectful, never clinical or salesy.
- Plain, honest language in English and Tamil; no invented claims or proof.
- Every treatment page explains the cause and the plan, not only the symptom.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No product-specific standard established. Tamil and English must both render and read well.
