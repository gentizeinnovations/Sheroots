Who it may suit

  • People whose joint pain limits exercise
  • Those returning after injury or pregnancy
  • Anyone unsure how to start
  • People who want a structured plan

How physiotherapy helps

  • A safe, progressive exercise plan
  • Strengthening that protects your joints
  • Activity pacing advice
  • Regular check-ins to keep you on track

Common reasons people come to us

  • Joint or back pain that makes exercise difficult
  • Weight gained during pregnancy or after an injury
  • Busy routines with little time for activity
  • Hormonal conditions such as PCOS or thyroid problems, managed with your doctor
  • Not knowing where to start or how to stay consistent

What Weight Loss Physiotherapy sessions involve

We look at your health history, any pain or joint problems, and your current activity level. Together we build a safe, progressive plan mixing strength work and cardiovascular exercise that protects your joints, with regular check-ins to adjust the plan and keep you on track. We work alongside your doctor or dietitian where needed.

How long recovery can take

Healthy weight change is gradual. Many people notice better energy, strength and fitness within a few weeks of regular exercise, even before the scales change. Food and sleep play a big part too.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. We listenA relaxed conversation about your history, your goals and what worries you.
  2. We assessA careful assessment to find the cause, with every step explained first.
  3. We plan togetherHands-on care, guided exercise and a home plan made for you.

Simple things that help at home

  • Aim for regular movement on most days, even short walks
  • Add two or three strength sessions a week
  • Track your progress by how you feel and what you can do, not only weight
  • Pair exercise with balanced meals and good sleep

See a doctor first if you have

  • Chest pain, fainting or severe breathlessness during exercise
  • Rapid, unexplained weight loss or gain
  • New swelling of the legs

Weight Loss: common questions

Can physiotherapy alone make me lose weight?

Exercise is one part. Weight change also depends on diet, sleep and health conditions. We focus on safe, sustainable activity and can work alongside a dietitian.

I have knee pain. Can I still exercise?

Yes. We choose exercises that strengthen without overloading painful joints, and progress them gradually.

How often should I exercise?

Most adults benefit from regular movement on most days. We help you build up to a routine that fits your life.

Is this right for me?

Every plan starts with an assessment. We will tell you honestly if physiotherapy suits you, or if you should see another health professional.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and any scan reports, referral letters or a list of your medicines.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis or medical advice. Results vary from person to person. If you have severe or sudden symptoms, please seek urgent medical care.
