Carpal tunnel syndrome happens when a nerve at the wrist is squeezed, causing hand symptoms. Wrist pain can also come from overuse.

Signs you may notice

  • Tingling or numbness in the fingers, often at night
  • A weak grip
  • An ache in the wrist
  • Dropping things

How physiotherapy helps

  • Advice on activity and hand use
  • Nerve-gliding and strengthening exercises
  • Guidance on splints where suitable
  • Workstation and daily-task advice

What can cause it?

  • Repeated wrist bending during typing, sewing, cutting or tool use
  • Pregnancy, when fluid changes can squeeze the nerve
  • Hormonal changes, thyroid problems or diabetes
  • Wrist injuries or arthritis
  • Holding a phone or steering wheel for long periods

What Wrist Pain & Carpal Tunnel Physiotherapy sessions involve

We check where your symptoms are, test grip and sensation, and look at the tasks that bring them on. Treatment can include nerve-gliding and strengthening exercises, advice on hand use and workstation set-up, and, where suitable, guidance on wearing a night splint to keep the wrist straight while you sleep.

How long recovery can take

Mild carpal tunnel symptoms often improve over several weeks with splinting and activity changes. Carpal tunnel during pregnancy often eases after birth. If symptoms are severe or getting worse, we will advise you to see your doctor.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. We listenA relaxed conversation about your history, your goals and what worries you.
  2. We assessA careful assessment to find the cause, with every step explained first.
  3. We plan togetherHands-on care, guided exercise and a home plan made for you.

Simple things that help at home

  • Keep your wrist straight when typing and sleeping
  • Take regular breaks from repetitive hand tasks
  • Shake out or gently move your hands when they tingle
  • Avoid gripping too hard on tools and phones

See a doctor first if you have

  • Constant numbness or weakness that is getting worse
  • Wasting of the muscles at the base of the thumb
  • Severe pain, swelling or deformity after a fall

Wrist Pain & Carpal Tunnel: common questions

Why is the tingling worse at night?

Many people bend their wrists while sleeping, which increases pressure on the nerve. A night splint can help keep the wrist straight.

Can pregnancy cause carpal tunnel?

Yes. Fluid changes in pregnancy can squeeze the nerve. It often settles after birth, and physiotherapy can ease symptoms meanwhile.

Will I need surgery?

Many people improve without surgery. If symptoms are severe or do not improve, your doctor may discuss other options.

Is this right for me?

Every plan starts with an assessment. We will tell you honestly if physiotherapy suits you, or if you should see another health professional.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and any scan reports, referral letters or a list of your medicines.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis or medical advice. Results vary from person to person. If you have severe or sudden symptoms, please seek urgent medical care.
