Diastasis Recti Correction in Madurai

டயஸ்டாசிஸ் ரெக்டி திருத்தம்

Diastasis recti is a separation of the tummy muscles along the midline. It is common during and after pregnancy.

Call us Book a session

Signs you may notice

  • A bulge or doming along the middle of the tummy
  • A weak core
  • Back pain
  • A feeling of a gap in the tummy

How physiotherapy helps

  • An assessment of your tummy muscles
  • Safe core exercises
  • Breathing and posture guidance
  • A graded return to activity

What can cause it?

  • Stretching of the tummy muscles during pregnancy
  • More than one pregnancy, or a large baby or twins
  • Straining, heavy lifting or a chronic cough
  • Weak deep core muscles
  • Returning to intense abdominal exercise too soon

What Diastasis Recti Correction sessions involve

We measure the gap and check how well the tissue between the muscles tenses, which matters as much as the width. Treatment focuses on deep core and breathing exercises, safe lifting and daily movement habits, and a graded return to the exercise you enjoy.

How long recovery can take

Some separation is normal after pregnancy and often improves on its own in the first months. With exercise, many women see gradual improvement in strength and function over a few months. A small gap may remain without causing problems.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. We listenA relaxed conversation about your history, your goals and what worries you.
  2. We assessA careful assessment to find the cause, with every step explained first.
  3. We plan togetherHands-on care, guided exercise and a home plan made for you.

Simple things that help at home

  • Roll onto your side to get out of bed
  • Breathe out and gently draw your tummy in when lifting
  • Avoid straining on the toilet
  • Pause full sit-ups and crunches until you have been assessed

See a doctor first if you have

  • A painful bulge that cannot be pushed back (possible hernia)
  • Severe tummy pain, vomiting or fever
  • Bleeding or discharge from a scar

Diastasis Recti Correction: common questions

How do I know if I have diastasis recti?

You may notice a bulge or 'doming' along the middle of your tummy when you sit up. An assessment can measure the gap and check the tissue.

Can diastasis recti improve years after pregnancy?

Yes. Core strength and function can improve with the right exercise at any time, even years later.

Should I avoid all abdominal exercise?

No. The right core exercises are an important part of recovery. We show you which ones to do now and which to build up to.

Is this right for me?

Every plan starts with an assessment. We will tell you honestly if physiotherapy suits you, or if you should see another health professional.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and any scan reports, referral letters or a list of your medicines.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis or medical advice. Results vary from person to person. If you have severe or sudden symptoms, please seek urgent medical care.
