Joint Pain physiotherapy in Madurai

Joint pain and stiffness can have many causes, including arthritis. Physiotherapy helps you keep moving and stay active.

Call us Book a session

Signs you may notice

  • Stiff joints, especially in the morning
  • Swelling
  • Pain with movement
  • Reduced range of movement

How physiotherapy helps

  • Gentle mobility work
  • Strengthening to support the joints
  • Activity and pacing advice
  • A joint-friendly exercise plan

What can cause it?

  • Osteoarthritis (age-related wear in the joints)
  • Inflammatory conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis, managed with your doctor
  • Previous injuries
  • Reduced activity and muscle weakness
  • Extra body weight placing more load on the joints

What Joint Pain Physiotherapy sessions involve

We assess which joints are affected, how they move and how they affect your daily life. Treatment combines gentle mobility work, strengthening to support the joints, and advice on pacing so you can stay active without flare-ups. If an inflammatory condition is suspected, we will advise you to see your doctor.

How long recovery can take

Joint pain linked to arthritis is usually managed over the long term. Many people find that regular, suitable exercise reduces pain and stiffness over a few months and helps them stay independent.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. We listenA relaxed conversation about your history, your goals and what worries you.
  2. We assessA careful assessment to find the cause, with every step explained first.
  3. We plan togetherHands-on care, guided exercise and a home plan made for you.

Simple things that help at home

  • Move little and often, especially after resting
  • Use warmth to ease morning stiffness
  • Balance activity with rest during flare-ups
  • Choose joint-friendly exercise such as walking or cycling

See a doctor first if you have

  • A single joint that becomes suddenly hot, red and swollen
  • Joint pain with fever or feeling generally unwell
  • Joint pain after a fall with inability to use the limb

Joint Pain: common questions

Is it safe to exercise with arthritis?

Yes. Suitable exercise is recommended for most types of arthritis. We tailor it to your joints and build it up slowly.

Why are my joints stiff in the morning?

Joints often stiffen after rest. Gentle movement usually helps. Long-lasting morning stiffness can be a sign of an inflammatory condition, which your doctor should check.

Can physiotherapy replace my medicines?

No. Physiotherapy works alongside the treatment your doctor prescribes. Always speak to your doctor before changing medicines.

Is this right for me?

Every plan starts with an assessment. We will tell you honestly if physiotherapy suits you, or if you should see another health professional.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and any scan reports, referral letters or a list of your medicines.
