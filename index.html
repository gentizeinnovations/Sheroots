Core Physiotherapy

Neck Pain & Cervical Spondylosis

கழுத்து வலி & செர்விகல் ஸ்பாண்டிலோசிஸ்

Neck pain is very common. It is often linked to long hours at a desk or on a phone, poor posture, or age-related changes in the neck joints (cervical spondylosis).

Signs you may notice
  • Stiffness or aching in the neck
  • Pain that spreads to the shoulders or arms
  • Headaches that start from the neck
  • Difficulty turning your head
How physiotherapy can help
  • Hands-on techniques to ease stiff joints and tight muscles
  • Targeted exercises for the neck and shoulder muscles
  • Advice on posture and workstation set-up
  • A home programme to keep you moving

Shoulder Pain & Frozen Shoulder

தோள்பட்டை வலி & உறைந்த தோள்பட்டை

Shoulder pain can follow overuse or injury, or come with stiffness such as frozen shoulder, where movement becomes painful and limited.

Signs you may notice
  • Pain when lifting your arm or reaching behind you
  • An ache that is worse at night
  • Stiffness and limited movement
  • Weakness when using the arm
How physiotherapy can help
  • Gentle mobilisation to improve movement
  • Graded exercises to rebuild strength
  • Techniques to ease pain
  • Guidance on everyday activities and pacing

Core Physiotherapy

Tennis Elbow

டென்னிஸ் எல்போ

Tennis elbow is pain on the outer side of the elbow caused by overuse of the forearm tendons. It is not only from tennis: gripping, typing and lifting can all contribute.

Signs you may notice
  • Pain on the outside of the elbow
  • A weak grip
  • Pain lifting a cup or turning a door handle
  • Tenderness when pressing near the elbow
How physiotherapy can help
  • Advice on managing load and everyday tasks
  • Progressive strengthening exercises
  • Soft-tissue techniques
  • Guidance on grip and workstation set-up

Core Physiotherapy

Back Pain & Sciatica

முதுகு வலி & சியாட்டிகா

Back pain is one of the most common reasons people see a physiotherapist. Sciatica is pain that travels from the lower back or buttock down the leg, often from an irritated nerve.

Signs you may notice
  • Aching or sharp pain in the lower back
  • Pain, tingling or numbness down the leg
  • Stiffness after sitting
  • Pain when bending or lifting
How physiotherapy can help
  • Hands-on treatment to ease pain and stiffness
  • Exercises to move and strengthen your back and core
  • Education on pacing and returning to activity
  • Advice on lifting, sitting and sleeping positions

முழங்கால் வலி & மூட்டு தேய்மானம்

Knee pain can come from an injury, overuse, or wear-and-tear changes in the joint (osteoarthritis).

Signs you may notice

  • Clicking or a feeling of giving way
How physiotherapy can help
  • Strengthening for the hip and knee
  • Exercises to keep the joint moving
  • Guidance on activity and pacing
  • Walking and movement advice

Ankle Sprain

கணுக்கால் சுளுக்கு

An ankle sprain happens when the ligaments are stretched or torn, usually after a twist or awkward landing.

Signs you may notice
  • Pain and swelling around the ankle
  • Difficulty walking
  • Bruising
  • A feeling that the ankle is unstable
How physiotherapy can help

Posture Correction

தோரணை திருத்தம்

Long hours sitting, looking at screens or carrying children can strain the body over time. Posture work looks at how you sit, stand and move.

Signs you may notice
  • Rounded shoulders or a head that sits forward
  • Neck or back aching by the end of the day
  • Tight chest or hips
  • Tiredness from holding positions
How physiotherapy can help
  • An assessment of how you sit, stand and move
  • Exercises to strengthen and stretch the right areas
  • Practical workstation and daily-habit advice
  • A simple routine you can keep up

Sports Injuries

விளையாட்டு காயங்கள்

Sprains, strains and overuse problems can happen in any sport or training. Rehab helps you recover and get back with confidence.

Signs you may notice
  • Sudden pain during activity
  • Swelling or bruising
  • Pain that comes back when you train
  • A drop in performance
How physiotherapy can help
  • Assessment of the injury and how you move

  • Strength and conditioning for return to activity
  • Advice to help reduce the chance of it happening again

Wrist Pain & Carpal Tunnel

மணிக்கட்டு வலி & கார்பல் டன்னல்

Carpal tunnel syndrome happens when a nerve at the wrist is squeezed, causing hand symptoms. Wrist pain can also come from overuse.

Signs you may notice
  • Tingling or numbness in the fingers, often at night
  • A weak grip
  • An ache in the wrist
  • Dropping things
How physiotherapy can help
  • Advice on activity and hand use
  • Nerve-gliding and strengthening exercises
  • Guidance on splints where suitable
  • Workstation and daily-task advice

Cardiac Rehabilitation

இதய மறுவாழ்வு

Cardiac rehabilitation is a programme of exercise and education that helps people recover after a heart event or procedure. Your doctor's advice always comes first.

Who it may suit
  • People recovering from a heart attack
  • After heart surgery or a stent
  • Living with a heart condition
  • Anyone who wants to be active safely
How physiotherapy can help
  • Gradual exercise suited to you
  • Attention to how you feel during activity
  • Education on activity and healthy habits
  • Support to build your confidence

Pelvic Floor Rehabilitation

இடுப்புத் தள மறுவாழ்வு

The pelvic floor muscles support the bladder, bowel and uterus. They can weaken or become tight after pregnancy and childbirth, with hormonal changes, or with strain. You are welcome to ask us anything before you book.

Signs you may notice
  • Leaking when you cough, sneeze or exercise
  • A sudden urgent need to go
  • A feeling of heaviness or dragging
  • Pelvic pain or pain during intimacy
How physiotherapy can help
  • A private assessment with clear explanations
  • An individual exercise programme
  • Relaxation and breathing techniques
  • Guidance for exercise and daily life

PCOS / PCOD Physiotherapy

PCOS/PCOD பிசியோ

PCOS/PCOD is a hormonal condition diagnosed and managed by your doctor. Regular physical activity is commonly recommended as part of day-to-day management, and physiotherapy can help you build a plan that suits you.

Who it may suit
  • Women diagnosed by their doctor with PCOS/PCOD
  • Anyone unsure what exercise is right for them
  • Those who feel tired or find it hard to stay consistent
  • People who want a structured programme

  • A tailored exercise programme
  • A balance of strength and cardiovascular work

Dry Needling Therapy

ட்ரை நீட்லிங் தெரபி

Dry needling uses fine needles to target tight spots in muscles, and is used alongside other physiotherapy. We explain what it involves and gain your consent before anything starts.

Who it may suit
  • Muscle knots or persistent tightness
  • Neck, shoulder or back muscle pain
  • Active people with tight muscles
  • Anyone who has been advised it may suit them
How physiotherapy can help
  • An assessment first, to see if it suits you
  • Used as part of a wider plan
  • Combined with movement and exercise
  • A clear explanation of what to expect

Diastasis Recti Correction

டயஸ்டாசிஸ் ரெக்டி திருத்தம்

Diastasis recti is a separation of the tummy muscles along the midline. It is common during and after pregnancy.

Signs you may notice
  • A bulge or doming along the middle of the tummy
  • A weak core
  • Back pain
  • A feeling of a gap in the tummy
How physiotherapy can help
  • An assessment of your tummy muscles
  • Safe core exercises
  • Breathing and posture guidance
  • A graded return to activity
