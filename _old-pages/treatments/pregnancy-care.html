Pregnancy (Antenatal) Care

கர்ப்பகால பராமரிப்பு

Pregnancy changes your posture, joints and core. Physiotherapy supports your comfort and helps you prepare for birth and recovery.

Signs you may notice

  • Low back or pelvic girdle pain
  • Hip pain
  • Trouble sleeping or moving comfortably
  • Worry about exercising safely

How physiotherapy can help

  • Safe exercise guidance
  • Techniques to ease pain
  • Exercises to prepare for birth
  • Advice on posture and daily tasks

What to expect

  1. We listen. A relaxed conversation about your history, goals and concerns.
  2. We assess. A careful assessment, with every step explained.
  3. We plan together. Treatment and exercises tailored to you, with a home plan.

Common questions

Is this right for me?

Every plan starts with an assessment. We will tell you honestly if physiotherapy suits you, or if you should see another health professional.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition, your goals and how your body responds. We will talk through a likely plan after your first assessment.

Do I need a referral?

Please call us and we will confirm for your situation.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and any scan reports, referral letters or a list of your medicines.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis or medical advice. Results vary from person to person. If you have severe or sudden symptoms, seek urgent medical care.
